The Process

Forestry mulching uses a specialized machine with a rotating drum covered in steel teeth to grind vegetation — including trees, brush, and stumps — into small pieces. The mulched material is left on the ground as a protective layer.

Unlike traditional land clearing, there's no need to burn debris, haul it away, or deal with exposed soil. The process is faster, more eco-friendly, and leaves your land ready to use immediately.

Most residential projects are completed in a single day. Larger properties or dense overgrowth may take longer, but we'll give you a timeline when we provide your quote. Either way, forestry mulching is significantly faster than traditional clearing methods.

Our equipment can typically handle trees up to 8 inches in diameter. Larger trees may need to be cut down first or worked around, depending on your goals for the property. We'll discuss the best approach during our assessment.

Results & Aftermath

There is no debris to haul away — that's the beauty of forestry mulching. Everything gets ground into small pieces and left on the ground as mulch. This layer protects against erosion, retains moisture, and returns nutrients to the soil as it decomposes.

Some regrowth is natural over time, but the mulch layer significantly slows it down. Many plants won't return at all. For ongoing maintenance or particularly aggressive invasive species, we can discuss follow-up treatments or regular maintenance schedules.

Yes! The mulched area is ready for seeding, sodding, or landscaping. The mulch layer actually creates excellent conditions for new growth. For grass specifically, you may want to rake out some of the mulch or wait for it to decompose a bit, but the land is immediately usable.

We take care to minimize impact on your existing landscaping. Our equipment is designed to be relatively low-impact, and we plan access routes carefully. If you have specific concerns about access or sensitive areas, let us know and we'll work around them.
