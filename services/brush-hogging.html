Brush Hogging

Heavy-duty mowing for overgrown fields, pastures, and large open areas.

Reclaim Overgrown Open Land

When tall grass, weeds, and light brush have taken over your field or pasture, brush hogging is the efficient solution.

Our heavy-duty rotary mower cuts through vegetation that would destroy a regular lawn mower — tall grass, thick weeds, saplings, and light brush. Perfect for maintaining large open areas or preparing overgrown land for other uses.

Unlike forestry mulching (which handles dense brush and trees), brush hogging is designed for open areas with grass and lighter vegetation. It's faster and more economical for the right applications.

Best For:

  • Overgrown fields and pastures
  • Tall grass and weeds (3+ feet)
  • Light brush and saplings
  • Right-of-way maintenance
  • Annual property maintenance

Brush Hogging vs. Forestry Mulching

Not sure which service you need? Here's a simple guide.

Choose Brush Hogging When:

  • Land is mostly open (field, pasture)
  • Vegetation is grass, weeds, light brush
  • No significant trees or stumps
  • You need ongoing maintenance
  • Budget is a primary concern

Choose Forestry Mulching When:

  • Dense brush and trees present
  • Trees up to 8" diameter need clearing
  • Stumps need to be ground down
  • Invasive species removal needed
  • You want long-term results

Not sure? Contact us with photos and we'll recommend the right approach.
